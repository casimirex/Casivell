  Notes
  · Nominal figures. Pass --real to see them in today's purchasing power.
  · Rows past the marked line rest on projected statutory parameters, not law.
  · Points are Entgeltpunkte; Pension/mo is what that record would pay at the
    Rentenwert then in force, with no early-claim reduction applied.
  · Tax shown is withholding. The annual assessment settles the difference,
    and its refund or demand lands the following July in the Settle column.
  · Elterngeld is tax-free but raises the rate on the rest of the year's income
    (§ 32b EStG). That cost is inside the Settle column, a year later.
  · Kindererziehungszeit is credited only where --child-born says a child arrived;
    parental leave alone does not imply one, since either can occur without the other.
  · Not modelled: capital income, or income other than this employment.
